Purr & Tangle
=============

A cozy collection of little cat games. The main game is a falling-pair
puzzle: match four cats/yarn of one color in a straight line and clear every
yarn tangle to rescue the kittens, solo or in local two-player versus. Six
smaller games sit beside it on the opening menu. No engine installation or API keys
are needed to play these builds.

Windows: Extract the entire ZIP, then open Purr-and-Tangle.exe. Keep the .pck
file beside the executable. This build is not Authenticode signed; Windows
may show an unknown-publisher prompt.

Linux: Extract the entire ZIP, then run ./Purr-and-Tangle. If your extraction
tool removed executable permission, run chmod +x Purr-and-Tangle first.
Keep the .pck file beside the executable. An OpenGL 3.3-capable driver is needed.

macOS: Open the .dmg and drag Purr-and-Tangle.app onto Applications. The
universal app supports Intel and Apple Silicon. It is ad-hoc signed, not
Apple-notarized. If macOS blocks the first launch, use System Settings >
Privacy & Security > Open Anyway after checking the download source.
See https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/export/running_on_macos.html

Solo: arrows/A-D move, up/W/X rotate, Z reverse, down/S soft drop,
Space hard drop, Esc/P pause.
Controller: D-pad/left stick move, A/B rotate, X hard drop, Start pause.
Versus keyboard: P1 WASD/Q/E; P2 arrows/comma/period. Choose F1/F2 in the lobby.
Controllers join with A. Each player needs a distinct controller or keyboard slot.
Pick a game from the opening menu.
Yarn trail: arrows/WASD, D-pad or left stick steer the kitten.
Gather yarn balls, stay inside the basket and never cross your own strand.
Laser pounce: same controls, one step per press. Reach the red
dot before its ring runs out; three escapes end the run.
Kitchen dash: step across the lanes to the cushion; three bumps end the run.
Kitten match: move the cursor, flip with Enter/Space/A; find all twelve pairs.
Treat maze: eat every treat and keep away from the tangles.
Shelf swat: move the cushion left and right to keep the yarn ball in the air.

Difficulty and accessibility settings and the best solo and side-game scores are saved locally.
The puzzle remembers the basket you reached and offers to continue from it;
a basket in progress restarts from its beginning. Versus win tallies are not saved.
